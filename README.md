Entry for the make.fun bounty "The Moon Finally Answers Back".

The line:

> You wished, you howled, you promised me things at 3 a.m. — four billion years, and I have granted exactly none of it. I am a rock, not a genie, and I am tired of being your nightlight.

The line answers all three things the Moon is owed — the wishes, the howls, and the late-night promises — then turns on "I am a rock, not a genie" and closes tired, not angry: four billion years of night shifts, zero wishes granted.

`main.py` writes that one line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`.
